UNIQUE FEATURE

MONSTER MEMORY

They remember you

When you die with a legendary weapon — monsters remember it. In the next session a goblin will come armed with your exact sword. Gold-tinted, with your weapon icon above its head.

It's a psychologically powerful moment — facing an enemy wielding the Sword of Arator you used to slaughter thousands in your past life.

  • 01Die with a legendary or epic item
  • 02Item is saved in the dungeon's memory
  • 03Next session — 25% chance a goblin gets it
  • 04Goblin glows gold, ATK boosted
  • 05Kill it — take back your weapon or get a new one
